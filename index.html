Terroir, Craft & Sincerity

We view coffee not as a commodity, but as a botanical ritual.

Our beans originate from hyper-specific micro-climates in Huila (Colombia) and the high volcanic ridges of Guji (Ethiopia). Working strictly in direct trade relationships, we compensate farming collectives up to 300% above standard Fair Trade minimums.

Back inside our roastery, we apply sensory thermodynamic curves—preserving volatile esters rather than masking bean flaws behind carbonized heat. The result in your cup is crisp, sweet, expressive coffee that honours every hand in its journey.
